Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2, 2026

Welcome to TATE JALU VOYAGES. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website www.TateJaluVoyages.com and use our travel services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that identifies, relates to, or could reasonably be linked to you. This includes:

  • Personal Identifiers: Name, email address, phone number, billing address, and account login credentials.
  • Travel & Booking Details: Passport information (if required for international travel), frequent flyer numbers, and specific travel itinerary preferences.
  • Financial Data: Payment card numbers and billing details processed securely via our third-party payment gateways.
  • Technical and Usage Data: IP address, browser type, device info, and data on how you interact with our website via cookies.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the data we collect to provide a seamless travel-planning experience, specifically to:

  • Process and manage your bookings, account registration, and user profiles.
  • Send flight updates, confirmations, and administrative notifications.
  • Analyze website traffic and user behavior to optimize our landing page layout and performance.
  • Deliver promotional offers, newsletters, and marketing materials (if you have opted in).

3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

As noted on our entry page, we use cookies and similar tracking tools to analyze site performance, remember your preferences, and tailor content.

Accepting Cookies: Allows us to personalize your experience and remember your login state.

Rejecting Cookies: You can still browse our site, but certain dynamic features or booking flows may not function exactly as intended.

4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. We only share your information with trusted third parties under the following conditions:

  • Service Providers: Airlines, hotels, car rental agencies, and tour operators necessary to fulfill your booked travel itineraries.
  • Payment Processors: Secure third-party financial institutions that process your transactions.
  • Legal Compliance: If required by law, subpoena, or government enforcement agencies.

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard administrative, technical, and physical security measures designed to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, loss, or alteration. However, please note that no digital transmission or storage system can be guaranteed 100% secure.

6. Your Data Rights

Depending on your geographic location, you may have the following rights regarding your data:

  • The Right to Access: Request copies of the personal data we hold about you.
  • The Right to Rectification: Request that we correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • The Right to Erasure: Request that we delete your personal data under certain conditions.
  • The Right to Opt-Out: Object to or opt out of targeted advertising.
