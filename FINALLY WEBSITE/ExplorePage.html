Durban

If you love warm weather, endless golden beaches, and amazing food, Durban is the place to be. Located right on the warm Indian Ocean, this friendly coastal city feels like summer all year round. A beautiful mix of traditional Zulu culture and vibrant Indian influences, which you can feel in the colorful local markets. A massive beachfront promenade perfect for walking, biking, surfing, or just watching the sunrise.

Johannesburg

This is South Africa’s largest and most energetic city. It is a bustling hub of art, history, and urban culture, where there is always something happening around every corner. A fast-paced destination known for its fantastic nightlife, world-class shopping, and great restaurants. The city is packed with trendy neighborhoods like Maboneng and Rosebank, featuring incredible street art, local fashion, and rooftop markets.

Cape Town

Cape Town is one of the most beautiful cities in the world. Nestled between a dramatic mountain range and two oceans, it is a stunning destination packed with outdoor adventure, gorgeous scenery, and a laid-back lifestyle. Just outside the city center, you'll find lush historic wine valleys and a fantastic food scene overlooking the ocean. From the trendy shores of Camps Bay to the penguin colony at Boulders Beach, the coastlines are absolutely spectacular. The city is framed by the famous, flat-topped Table Mountain, which you can hike or ride a cableway up for breathtaking views.
